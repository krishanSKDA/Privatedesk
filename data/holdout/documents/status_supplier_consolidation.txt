MERIDIAN TRADING LLC - PROJECT STATUS NOTE

Project: Packaging supplier consolidation
Report date: 2026-10-02
Project owner: Aisha Rahman, Procurement Officer
Overall status: AMBER

Summary
The goal is to reduce packaging and warehouse consumables suppliers from five to two by 2027-03-31. The savings target is 8% of annual packaging spend of USD 610,000, which is about USD 48,800 per year. Halcyon Packaging GmbH is the preferred supplier for boxes, stretch film and bubble wrap. Cedar Print & Label Co. remains under review for printed labels.

Supplier status
- Halcyon Packaging GmbH: preferred, H2 2026 price list accepted
- Cedar Print & Label Co.: under review after a six-day late delivery on PO-2026-0871 in September
- Rapidpak Trading: to be phased out by 2026-12-31
- Gulf Wrap Supplies: to be phased out by 2026-12-31
- Coastal Office Supplies LLC: out of scope (office supplies, not packaging)

Risks and issues
1. Moving all box volume to Halcyon increases lead times to 21 days for medium and large boxes, so safety stock must rise by about two weeks.
2. A fraudulent request to change Cedar's bank details was received on 2026-09-24 and rejected. Supplier bank detail changes now need phone confirmation from Finance.
3. The decision on Cedar is due by 2026-11-15. If Cedar is dropped, an alternative label printer must be qualified.

Next steps

- Send phase-out notices to Rapidpak Trading and Gulf Wrap Supplies (Aisha Rahman, 2026-10-31)
